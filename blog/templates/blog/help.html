Help

Answers to common questions about reading, writing, and managing your stories on Rozvi.

Getting started

Create a free account from Get started, then sign in any time from the Sign in link in the header.

Writing and publishing a story

Once signed in, click Write in the header to open the story editor. Give your story a title, choose a topic, and write your content — an excerpt and estimated read time are generated for you automatically if you leave them blank. You can edit or delete any story you've published from your profile page.

Searching for stories

Use the search box in the header to find stories by title, content, topic, or author. You can also search directly from the search page.

Claps and comments

Signed-in readers can clap for a story to show appreciation, and can leave comments and replies at the bottom of any story.

Resetting your password

Forgot your password? Use the password reset form to receive a reset link by email.
